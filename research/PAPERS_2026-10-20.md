## Gen129: front-running the Goldman roll (2010-2026, entirely after the paper's sample)
| Variant | DEV 2010-17 | TEST 2018-22 | HOLD 2023-26 | Gross (no costs) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 enter 10 days before, energy (primary) | -0.54 | -0.57 | -0.30 | 0.01 / -0.42 / -0.01 |
| R2 enter 5 days before, energy | -0.25 | -0.15 | 0.11 | 0.58 / 0.35 / 0.52 |
| R3 enter 10 days before, 12 commodities | -1.49 | -1.30 | -0.49 | - |

- **The effect has been arbitraged away.** Only a small residual remains in the last 5 days, and it does not pay for four contract legs a month. This is the paper's own limits-to-arbitrage story.
- **Method:** the calendar spread is measured on the same pair of contracts. If the pair stops being observable, the trade closes at the previous close (no look-ahead in which months count).

## Round 3 papers (2026-10-20)
- **FOMC dates:** 136 statement dates 2009-2026 from federalreserve.gov. Most years have 8; seven meetings are missing in 2017/2018/2020/2023 because of page-format parsing gaps. That loses sample but adds no bias.
- **Gen131 F1, post-FOMC drift in ZN/ZB (Indriawan, Jiao and Tse 2021):** 124 events, 0.60 / -0.63 / 0.30. **Rejected.**
- **Bakas and Triantafyllou (2019), macro uncertainty -> commodity volatility:** a volatility forecast, not a return or direction signal. Only possibly useful for sizing; our books already target volatility. Not tested.
- **Smales (2021), Treasury auctions and futures volatility:** prices fall on auction days, and a higher bid-to-cover lifts them. That reaction is immediate at the 13:00 ET result, so it cannot be captured with hourly bars. The tradable pre-auction leg was Gen130 (near-miss). Not tested further.
- **Duplicates:** Gornall et al. and Anastasopoulos et al. were already reviewed (round 2).
