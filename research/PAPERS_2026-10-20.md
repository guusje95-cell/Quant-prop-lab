## Gen129: front-running the Goldman roll (2010-2026, entirely after the paper's sample)
| Variant | DEV 2010-17 | TEST 2018-22 | HOLD 2023-26 | Gross (no costs) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 enter 10 days before, energy (primary) | -0.54 | -0.57 | -0.30 | 0.01 / -0.42 / -0.01 |
| R2 enter 5 days before, energy | -0.25 | -0.15 | 0.11 | 0.58 / 0.35 / 0.52 |
| R3 enter 10 days before, 12 commodities | -1.49 | -1.30 | -0.49 | - |

- **The effect has been arbitraged away.** Only a small residual remains in the last 5 days, and it does not pay for four contract legs a month. This is the paper's own limits-to-arbitrage story.
- **Method:** the calendar spread is measured on the same pair of contracts. If the pair stops being observable, the trade closes at the previous close (no look-ahead in which months count).
