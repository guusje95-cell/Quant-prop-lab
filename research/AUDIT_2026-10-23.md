## 4. What this means

- No look-ahead and no implementation error were found in the strategies or the paper trader (apart from 3.1, a data-feed
  measurement issue). The edge is not market beta and not a few lucky months.
- The two real risks are **2.1 (universe chosen today)** and **2.2 (the holdout was reused)**. Both push the true Sharpe down,
  not up; that is why the planning Sharpe is about 1.5-1.8 and why Gen321 and the paper run decide before any purchase.
- Order of fixes: 3.1 (index feed, after the user's push) -> Gen321 (survivorship) -> 2.4 refill (optional) -> 2.7 day boundary in the EA.
