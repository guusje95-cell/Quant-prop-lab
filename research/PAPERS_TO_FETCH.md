# Papers to download (University of Twente library access)

Full texts give exact rules, parameters and cost assumptions. The research cloud can only see abstracts.

**How to share them:** upload the PDFs in the chat. Do not commit them to the repository, because of copyright.

## Crypto (to sharpen tests already run or near-misses)
1. Weiss, Gaudiosi, Zhou & Webb (2026), *Finance Research Letters*, DOI 10.1016/j.frl.2026.110340: Bitcoin options expiry and returns. Needed for the exact windows, open-interest conditioning and costs.
2. Bui & Nguyen (2026), arXiv 2602.11708: 6-hour crypto trend with coin selection. Free on arXiv.
3. Dhanya M.D. (2026?), SSRN 7301919: own-history factor ranking in crypto.
4. He, Manela, Ross & von Wachter (2022/2024), "Fundamentals of Perpetual Futures", arXiv 2212.06888.
5. Fieberg, Liedtke, Poddig, Walker & Zaremba (2025), *JFQA*: "A Trend Factor for the Cross-Section of Cryptocurrency Returns" (CTREND).
6. Borri, Liu, Tsyvinski & Wu (2025): cryptocurrency carry / basis.
7. Guo, Sang, Tu & Wang (2024), *Journal of Economic Dynamics and Control*: lead-lag in cryptocurrencies.
8. Zarattini, Pagani & Barbon (2025), SSRN: "Catching Crypto Trends" (daily and intraday ensembles).
9. Gornall, Rinaldi & Xiao (SSRN 5036933): perpetual futures drawdowns and liquidations.
