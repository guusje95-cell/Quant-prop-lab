On a BREAK funded account, the consistency rule is a 40% limit on how much of your total profits can come from a single trading day.

So, when you request a payout, we check that no one day's profit is more than 40% of your total profits to date.

Example: if your total profits are $5,000, your best day can be at most $2,000 (40%). If one day is over $2,000, you just need to keep trading until your profits are more evenly distributed, then request the payout again.

It's only checked at payout time, and it does not stop you from trading in the meantime.
