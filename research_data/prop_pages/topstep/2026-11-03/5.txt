September 10, 2026 Table of contents The goal is Live. The Trading Combine® earns you entry. The Express Funded Account is where you take Payouts and build the track record to get to the Live Funded Account. 3 stages, 1 destination — protect your account at every step. Trading Combine® The Trading Combine® evaluates your skills, discipline, and habits before you’re funded. It requires a monthly subscription. Objectives: Reach and maintain the Profit Target Consistency Target: best trading day must stay below 55% of total profits Rule: Do not let your Account Balance hit or exceed the Maximum Loss Limit (MLL) Express Funded Account™ — Standard Earn this by passing the Trading Combine®. No monthly subscription required. Objectives: 5 Winning Days of $150 or more (non-consecutive) to request a Payout Net profit greater than $0 since your last Payout Follow the Scaling Plan Rule: Do not let your Account Balance hit or exceed the Maximum Loss Limit Express Funded Account™ — Consistency An alternative XFA path with a different Payout structure. No monthly subscription required. Objectives: Trade at least 3 days at 40% consistency to request a Payout Follow the Scaling Plan Rule: Do not let your Account Balance hit or exceed the Maximum Loss Limit Live Funded Account® Topstep’s prop firm capital. Called up by the Risk Team after consistent XFA performance.
