Copy Trading Private WebSocket： wss://openapi.blofin.com/ws/copytrading/private Multi-Position Mode Multi-Position mode allows each opening order to create an independent position with its own positionId , entry price, margin, leverage, and TP/SL. Positions on the same instrument and direction are not merged. Multi-Position is an account-level setting, available under Hedge Mode ( long_short_mode ) only. See Set Position Mode . Both cross and isolated margin modes are supported. Sub-account settings are independent of the main account. Up to 10 positions per instrument. Orders exceeding the limit are rejected. In Multi-Position mode, closing orders and Close Positions requests must specify positionId . Opening orders may specify positionId to add to an existing position, or omit it to open a new position. Pending add-position orders are automatically canceled when the target position is fully closed or liquidated. reduceOnly is not supported under Hedge Mode and is ignored if passed. Leverage has two levels: instrument-level leverage (set without positionId ) applies to newly opened positions only and can be modified while holding positions; position-level leverage (set with positionId ) adjusts a single position. Positions on the same instrument and direction may hold different leverage. Accounts that do not enable Multi-Position are not affected. New response fields return an empty string. positionId is an opaque identifier. Do not parse or derive meaning from its structure. Trailing Stop Order A trailing stop order is an algo order that tracks favorable price movements and automatically submits a market order when the price retraces by a preset callback amount. It is built on the existing algo order framework — no new endpoints or channels are introduced. See Place Algo Order . Activation. If activePrice is not specified, the order starts tracking immediately upon placement. If specified, tracking starts once the last price reaches activePrice — the direction is not validated: the order is activated when the last price touches activePrice from either side. Both activation and tracking use the last price. Tracking. After activation, the system records the extreme price: the highest price H for sell orders, or the lowest price L for buy orders. Trigger. A sell order is triggered when the last price ≤ H × (1 − callbackRatio) , or ≤ H − callbackSpread . A buy order is triggered when the last price ≥ L × (1 + callbackRatio) , or ≥ L + callbackSpread . Execution. Once triggered, a market order is submitted. Trailing stop orders are always executed at market price; the orderPrice parameter is not applicable and is ignored if passed. In extreme market conditions, the final execution price may differ significantly from the trigger price. Trailing stop orders do not expire. They remain active until triggered or canceled. Each trading account may have a maximum of 50 pending (untriggered) partial-position trailing stop orders, of which at most 10 per instrument. In addition, each position may have 1 full-position ( size=-1 ) trailing stop order, which does not count toward the 50-order limit. Trailing stop orders and trigger orders are managed separately. When querying, amending, or canceling a trailing stop order, orderType=trailing_stop must always be passed. If orderType is not specified, it defaults to trigger : the request will be validated against trigger-order rules or fail to find the order. Trailing stop orders reuse the existing algo order state enumeration: State Description live Order placed. Not yet activated ( moveTriggerPrice is empty), or activated and tracking ( moveTriggerPrice is non-empty) effective Triggered. The market order has been submitted canceled Canceled by the user, or by the system (e.g. the associated position was fully closed) order_failed Triggered but the market order failed (e.g. insufficient margin) The triggered market order. The market order generated upon triggering enters the regular order system and may be split into multiple orders. It is pushed via the private WS Order Channel . The algo order response does not include the child order IDs; correlate child orders by instId and the triggering time window. System cancellation. When the associated position reaches zero (fully closed, liquidated, or ADL), the reduce-side trailing stop order is automatically canceled. cancelType does not distinguish position-closure cleanup from other system cancellations. Upon receiving a system cancellation, check your position: if it is non-zero, your position is no longer protected — re-place the trailing stop order immediately. Error handling. Errors are returned at two levels. Parameter-validation errors (missing or mutually exclusive parameters) are returned at the top level, e.g. code=152422 with no data . Business rejections may return code=0 at the top level with the failure carried in data.code / data.msg , e.g. data.code=148083 with algoId=null . Always check both the top-level code and data.code . Code Message Description 152422 Exactly one of callbackRatio and callbackSpread must be provided callbackRatio and callbackSpread are both passed, or neither is passed. Returned at the top level. 148082 Callback percentage range 0.1% - 100% callbackRatio out of range ( 0.001 – 1 ). Returned in data.code . 148083 Callback constant range callbackSpread out of range (0.1% – 100% of the last price). The message carries the valid range, e.g. Callback constant range 155.2 - 155,114.8 . Returned in data.code . Exceeding the pending order limit — 50 partial-position trailing stop orders per account, at most 10 per instrument, or a full-position trailing stop that already exists on the position — is rejected. Multi-Position mode errors (missing or invalid positionId ) reuse the unified Multi-Position error codes. A connection between the client and server can be established with just one handshake. The server will then be able to push data to the client according to preset rules. Its advantages include: The WebSocket request header size for data transmission between client and server is only 2 bytes. Either the client or server can initiate data transmission. There’s no need to repeatedly create and delete TCP connections, saving resources on bandwidth and server. We recommend developers use WebSocket API to retrieve market data and order book depth. When a request is rejected by our system due to rate limits, the system will return error code 429 (Rate limit reached. Please refer to API documentation and throttle requests accordingly). REST API Limits Limit the call of the endpoint by IP, up to 500 requests per minute, if triggered, the service will be suspended for 5 minutes; up to 1500 requests per 5 minutes, if triggered, the service will be suspended for 1 hour. Rate limits of trading-related APIs at 30 requests every 10 seconds (based on UserId) WebSocket Connection Management Connection Limits New Connections : 1 per second per IP Channel Types : Public channels via public service endpoint Private channels via private service endpoint If there’s a network problem, the system will automatically disable the connection. The connection will break automatically if the subscription is not established or data has not been pushed for more than 30 seconds. To keep the connection stable: 1.Set a timer of N seconds whenever a response message is received, where N is less than 30. 2.If the timer is triggered, which means that no new message is received within N seconds, send the String ‘ping’. 3.Expect a ‘pong’ as a response. If the response message is not received within N seconds, please raise an error or reconnect. Risk Control Restrictions BloFin has two types of risk control strategies for APIs: rate limit, network firewall restrictions. Network Firewall Restrictions Currently, we do not provide explicit information about network firewall restrictions. If you receive an HTTP 403 error message, it means you have violated a network firewall rule. In most cases, this error occurs due to excessive requests and will result in a five-minute temporary ban. If your requests are deemed malicious, it could result in an extended ban or potentially even a permanent suspension. Public Data The API endpoints of Public Data do not require authentication. Range: 0.001 (0.1%) – 1 (100%), step 0.001 . Either callbackRatio or callbackSpread is required, and only one of them can be passed. callbackSpread String Conditional Callback price distance (absolute price). Must conform to the tick size of the instrument. Valid range: 0.1% – 100% of the last price at placement, aligned with the callbackRatio range; out-of-range values are rejected with error 148083 . For trailing stop orders, size must conform to the lotSize / minSize of the instrument, and -1 represents the entire position: the triggered market order is placed with the actual position size at the moment of triggering. positionId is required in Multi-Position mode and must match the instId , marginMode and positionSide of the target position. Validation rules Either callbackRatio or callbackSpread must be passed, but not both; otherwise error 152422 is returned at the top level. activePrice direction is not validated. Make sure the activation price is on the intended side of the current price: e.g. a sell trailing stop with activePrice below the current price will stay dormant until the price falls to it, which is usually not the intended protection. Trailing stop orders are executed at market price only. The orderPrice parameter is not applicable and is ignored if passed. reduceOnly is not validated at placement. A reduce-only trailing stop order without a corresponding position is accepted and remains pending; if no position exists at the moment of triggering, the order fails ( order_failed ).
