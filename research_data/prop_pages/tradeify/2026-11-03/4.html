Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between futures and the stock market?

With stocks, you're buying a piece of one company and hoping it goes up. Futures are a different game. You can trade the price of gold, oil, the S&P 500, even the dollar itself, and you can make money whether the market's flying or falling. They run nearly around the clock too, so you're not stuck waiting for an opening bell.

Can I lose more than my initial investment?

No. You're trading on simulated capital, so the money in your bank account is never on the line. The worst that happens is you hit your account's loss limit and the account closes. That's it. No surprise bills, no calls asking you to cover a loss. Just your skills being tested, not your savings.

What hours are futures open?

Pretty much all of them. Markets run close to 23 hours a day, Sunday evening right through Friday afternoon. So whether you're trading with your morning coffee or sneaking in a session at 2am, the market's awake when you are.

What is a prop firm?

It's short for proprietary trading firm, and the idea is simple. We put up the capital so you can trade bigger than your own wallet would ever allow. You show us you can trade by passing an evaluation, we fund you, and you keep most of what you make. You bring the skill, we bring the money.

Do I need experience to start?

Not at all. Brand new and been-doing-this-for-years traders both kick off the same way, with a low-cost evaluation where you can find your feet without real money on the line. Nobody's born knowing how to trade. You learn it by doing it, and this is a safe place to put in the reps.

What happens if I break a rule?

Nothing scary. Every account comes with clear rules laid out from day one, like a daily loss limit, and if you cross one, the account just ends. There's no hidden penalty and nobody chasing you for cash. You reset, you figure out what went sideways, and you come back sharper. The rules are there to build good habits, not to trip you up.

How do I get a funded account?

Pick the account size you want, then pass the evaluation by hitting a profit target while staying inside the rules. That's the whole thing. No interview, no CV, no committee deciding your fate. Prove you can trade and the capital is yours.

What's tradable on the futures market?

Honestly, most of what moves the world. Stock indices like the S&P 500 and Nasdaq, metals like gold and silver, energy like crude oil and natural gas, plus currencies, bonds, and even crops. One account, and the whole global market is in front of you.
