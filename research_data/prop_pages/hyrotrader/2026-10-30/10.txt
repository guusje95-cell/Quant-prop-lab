Can I use trading bots or signal bots during the challenge or on a funded account? Yes. HyroTrader allows trading bots, as long as the strategy is your own. You can develop your own trading bot or build your own automated strategy, and connect it to your challenge: Bybit challenges: connect your bot directly to the Bybit demo API. Hyro challenges: connect your bot to the Hyro platform. BloFin challenges: connect your bot to the BloFin demo API. Copying someone else's strategy is not allowed. This includes ready-made bots running another person's strategy, copy-trading services, account mirroring and external signal providers. All bots must follow our trading rules. Setups that don't comply may lead to an account review, account restrictions or disqualification. Backed by the community All systems operational Company Evaluation Documentation All trading on HyroTrader takes place on simulated (demo) accounts that run on real exchange market data. There is no real-capital stage at any point. HyroTrader is not a broker, does not accept deposits for trading, and does not provide investment services, investment advice or portfolio management. Where you connect your own Bybit or BloFin demo account, that account is yours; the simulated balance HyroTrader allocates to a challenge or funded account is not, and a payout is a contractual share of simulated trading performance. Where you trade through a third-party exchange or terminal, you must meet that provider's own terms and eligibility rules and comply with the law of your country. The Tealstreet and Tiger terminals are not available to traders in the United States or in countries where Bybit is restricted, and you must not use a VPN or any other means to get around that. The evaluation is a genuine test of risk management, and many traders do not pass. HyroTrader's revenue comes from challenge fees. Trading financial markets carries high risk. Nothing on this site is a recommendation to trade, and it is not directed at residents of any jurisdiction where that would be contrary to local law.
