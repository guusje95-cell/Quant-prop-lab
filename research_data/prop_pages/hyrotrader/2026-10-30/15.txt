You can connect your challenge to Claude, or any other AI assistant that supports MCP (Model Context Protocol). Your AI can then check your challenges, results and trade history, and trade for you if you allow it. How to set it up Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) in your HyroTrader account. It's required before you can create a token. Go to Settings → Agent tokens and create a token. Read access lets your AI view your account; turn on trading access if you want it to place trades. Add the HyroTrader MCP server and your token to your AI assistant's MCP settings. This is available only for challenges on the Hyro platform, not for Bybit, BloFin or other trading terminals. A full step-by-step setup guide is coming soon. Keep your token private: anyone who has it can access your challenge. You can revoke a token at any time in Settings → Agent tokens.
