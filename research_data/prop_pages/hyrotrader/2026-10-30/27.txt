What are prohibited trading actions? Capital Limit Your total allocation may be up to $400,000 across evaluation challenges and $200,000 across funded accounts. Exceeding either limit is not permitted. Prohibited Actions – Bybit Challenge The following actions are not allowed: Requesting additional demo funds Modifying the USDT balance Deleting or altering the API key Trading spot in a futures-based challenge Trading the EUR/USD pair Trading options or USDC pairs Combining spot and margin trading on the same account Violation of any of the above may result in account failure or termination. Backed by the community All systems operational Company Evaluation Documentation All trading on HyroTrader takes place on simulated (demo) accounts that run on real exchange market data. There is no real-capital stage at any point. HyroTrader is not a broker, does not accept deposits for trading, and does not provide investment services, investment advice or portfolio management. Where you connect your own Bybit or BloFin demo account, that account is yours; the simulated balance HyroTrader allocates to a challenge or funded account is not, and a payout is a contractual share of simulated trading performance. Where you trade through a third-party exchange or terminal, you must meet that provider's own terms and eligibility rules and comply with the law of your country. The Tealstreet and Tiger terminals are not available to traders in the United States or in countries where Bybit is restricted, and you must not use a VPN or any other means to get around that. The evaluation is a genuine test of risk management, and many traders do not pass. HyroTrader's revenue comes from challenge fees. Trading financial markets carries high risk. Nothing on this site is a recommendation to trade, and it is not directed at residents of any jurisdiction where that would be contrary to local law.
