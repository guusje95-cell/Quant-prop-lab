Tips for Staying Compliant

Follow these best practices to trade within the rules and protect your funded account.

1

Use proper risk management

Define your risk per trade in advance. Stop-loss orders are not required, but we recommend using them.

2

Monitor total exposure

Track your cumulative open positions to ensure you stay within allowed exposure and leverage limits.

3

Spread your profit across days

On One-Step and Two-Step, no single trading day counts for more than 40% of the target (on Zero-Step, no single trade for more than 50%).

4

Maintain consistent performance

Trade steadily and avoid erratic behavior that could breach evaluation or drawdown requirements.

All trading on HyroTrader takes place on simulated (demo) accounts that run on real exchange market data. There is no real-capital stage at any point. Where you connect your own Bybit or BloFin demo account, that account is yours; the simulated balance HyroTrader allocates to a challenge or funded account is not, and a payout is a contractual share of simulated trading performance. The Tealstreet and Tiger terminals are not available to traders in the United States or in countries where Bybit is restricted, and you must not use a VPN or any other means to get around that. The evaluation is a genuine test of risk management, and many traders do not pass. HyroTrader's revenue comes from challenge fees. Trading financial markets carries high risk.
