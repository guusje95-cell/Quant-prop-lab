Who can join HyroTrader? At HyroTrader, we welcome traders from around the world, except residents and citizens of the prohibited jurisdictions listed in our Terms and Conditions . We believe in providing equal opportunities to individuals who possess the necessary trading skills and mindset. There are no specific qualifications or educational backgrounds required to join our platform. To be eligible to join HyroTrader, all prospective clients must be at least 18 years old. We prioritize traders who have experience and a proven track record in trading cryptocurrencies or forex. We value profitable trading strategies and sound risk management practices. Whether you’ve been trading cryptocurrencies or forex for a considerable period or have recently achieved success. Your ability to trade profitably and effectively manage risks is what matters to us. Join our diverse community of traders at HyroTrader, and unlock the potential to further enhance your trading career. Benefit from our supportive environment and educational resources. Backed by the community All systems operational Company Evaluation Documentation All trading on HyroTrader takes place on simulated (demo) accounts that run on real exchange market data. There is no real-capital stage at any point. HyroTrader is not a broker, does not accept deposits for trading, and does not provide investment services, investment advice or portfolio management. Where you connect your own Bybit or BloFin demo account, that account is yours; the simulated balance HyroTrader allocates to a challenge or funded account is not, and a payout is a contractual share of simulated trading performance. Where you trade through a third-party exchange or terminal, you must meet that provider's own terms and eligibility rules and comply with the law of your country. The Tealstreet and Tiger terminals are not available to traders in the United States or in countries where Bybit is restricted, and you must not use a VPN or any other means to get around that. The evaluation is a genuine test of risk management, and many traders do not pass. HyroTrader's revenue comes from challenge fees. Trading financial markets carries high risk. Nothing on this site is a recommendation to trade, and it is not directed at residents of any jurisdiction where that would be contrary to local law.
