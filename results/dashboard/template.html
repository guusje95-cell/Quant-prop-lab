Gen53: new research

Each idea came from published research and was written down before testing. Point-in-time universes pick, every month, the coins with the highest trading volume at that time, including coins that later collapsed. That removes the survivorship bias of choosing today's winners.

How to read this

  • Gen53 found one real upgrade: C* + BTC leads (hold altcoins only while Bitcoin itself trends up). Sharpe 1.78 vs 1.69, test period 1.63 vs 1.50, smaller drawdown, and it also helps on bias-free point-in-time universes and in both prop firms on hourly prices.
  • Survivorship check: on point-in-time universes C* earns about 1.3–1.45 Sharpe instead of 1.7. Plan with those lower numbers.
  • Catching Crypto Trends, an independent design from a 2025 paper, reaches Sharpe 1.44 on the same coins. That supports the trend edge being real.
  • For Crypto Fund Trader 1-Phase, plain C* (challenge 1.0×, funded 0.4×) was not beaten once the hourly replay was applied.
  • For FTMO 2-Step, the optimised setup (no stablecoin filter, 20-day volatility, 0.2 no-trade band, 1.5× challenge, 0.6× funded, daily stop at 90% of the limit, 4% take-profit) earned more on test and on hourly prices. It was picked among about 5,900 trials, so paper trade it first.
  • Past results do not guarantee future payouts. Firm rules and fees come from public sources and change.
